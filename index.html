EpiLog

What this is

EpiLog is a shared, pilot-stage registry of gene-editing loci and their expression outcome over time — mainly post-insertion epigenetic silencing. It is maintained for internal research use among invited labs.

What you agree to by using this

Data you submit (locus, species, edit type, check-in results) becomes part of a shared database, used for aggregate analysis and to improve the automatic annotations returned to all users.

You may not extract, redistribute, republish, or substantially reuse data obtained through this platform outside of it, in whole or in part, without prior written permission. This database is a protected compilation (including under the EU's sui generis database right); unauthorized extraction or reuse of a substantial part of its content constitutes infringement.

There is no guarantee of accuracy for aggregated data — this is a collaborative, beta-stage registry, not a clinically validated resource.

Questions or data removal

Contact the project lead for removal requests or clarifications.

EpiLog maintains a curated, literature-derived catalog of genomic safe harbor loci and their post-editing expression/silencing outcomes. The interactive table below loads live from the database once you search or filter; for reference, some of the loci currently catalogued include:

  • AAVS1 (human, 19q13.4) — the most extensively characterized human safe harbor; multiple studies show robust iPSC expression but tissue-dependent silencing after cardiomyocyte differentiation.
  • ROSA26 (human, mouse, pig, goat, cattle orthologs) — a broadly conserved "quiet locus" used across species for stable transgene expression.
  • H11 (human, mouse, pig, goat orthologs) — intergenic locus between DRG1/EIF4ENIF1, validated for neuronal differentiation and stable multi-species transgene expression.
  • CCR5 (human) — HIV-resistance-linked locus; natural CCR5-delta32 loss is well tolerated but trades HIV resistance for increased West Nile virus susceptibility.
  • CLYBL (human) — outperforms AAVS1 in several studies; endogenous gene has a real but tolerable metabolic role (vitamin B12 pathway).
  • TRAC, GSH6 (human) — genomic safe harbors used in CAR-T cell engineering, including real clinical trial data.
  • Rogi1, Rogi2, Pansio-1, Olonne-18, Keppel-19 (human, GRCh38 coordinates) — computationally discovered and experimentally validated safe harbor candidates.
  • attP2, attP40, ZH-attP-86Fb, VK00001–VK00040 (Drosophila melanogaster, dm3 coordinates) — phiC31-integrase docking sites for germline transgenesis; attP40 has a documented tissue-specific caveat (msp-300 disruption).
  • pH11 (pig) and caprine H11/ROSA26 (goat) — livestock safe harbors validated with per-tissue expression data across heart, liver, spleen, lung, kidney, muscle, and more.

Every entry is tagged with a confidence tier (how directly the data was extracted from the primary literature), a stability/duration summary, tissue context, and a linked PubMed source. The full catalog (currently well over 100 records) is also available as a raw, machine-readable JSON feed: view full catalog as JSON.
